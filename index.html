05 /A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What does INTERDEC do?

INTERDEC acquires land in Texas, develops it, and sells developed land. The company's full legal name is INTERNATIONAL TERRA DEVELOPING CORP, LLC.

Where is Puerta del Sol?

The groundbreaking invitation lists Salinas Blvd & Mile 10 N Rd, Donna, TX 78537. Confirm the exact site entrance with the project contact before visiting.

What types of lots are planned?

The invitation announces 49 residential lots and 3 commercial lots. Lot sizes, final plans, and current availability are still to be confirmed.

How do I get pricing and availability?

Use the contact section to prepare an inquiry. The number printed on the Puerta del Sol invitation is (956) 456-1788. Prices, financing terms, and confirmed inventory have not yet been provided for this website.

Can I discuss selling land to INTERDEC?

Yes. Select “I have land to sell” in the inquiry form and include the location, approximate acreage, and any useful property information to start the conversation.
